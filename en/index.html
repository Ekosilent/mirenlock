CleanCLI: ask for your data to be erased, from your own PC.

A Windows program that prepares data erasure requests (GDPR and similar laws) and newsletter unsubscribes. Nothing is sent without your confirmation.

What it does

Erasure requests

Prepares a formal request to the service's privacy contact, with the legal references and deadline of your country (EU/EEA, UK, US-California, Brazil), and follows it until a reply or the deadline.

Unsubscribes

One-Click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) only when the message has a valid DKIM signature and the link is HTTPS to a public host.

Guides for protected services

For Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon and the like it automates nothing: it shows you the step-by-step procedure.

Evidence log

Every send is recorded in a hash-chained log, exportable and verifiable by third parties. It detects edits and deletions.

Sites not in the list

You can add a site: the program reads a few public pages, finds the privacy notice and suggests the contact, which you confirm.

Italian and English

Guided menu in Italian and English. No commands to remember: it opens with a double click.

What it does not do, and where your data lives

  • Your data (name, e-mail, requests, log) stays only on your computer, encrypted. The key is protected by Windows (DPAPI).
  • It never logs in to your accounts at the services: no automated logins.
  • Every request has a preview and is sent only after your explicit confirmation.
  • It uses your own mailbox with the credentials you enter. Reading is read-only and local.
  • The program contacts Lemon Squeezy for the license and downloads signed data updates from a public file.

How it works

  1. Buy the license and download the program.
  2. Double-click cleancli.exe: pick a language and enter your license key.
  3. Enter your details and connect your mailbox (with a verification code).
  4. Choose a service, check the preview and confirm.

Limits, worth knowing

  • Outcomes depend on the services you write to and on applicable law: no guarantee of results.
  • Request templates, legal deadlines and contacts are informational and may change: you must check them. The program is not legal advice.
  • Windows only. The license covers a limited number of computers, stated at purchase.
  • Sites that block automated programs stay in manual mode.
