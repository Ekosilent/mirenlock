1. Data that stays on your computer. Name, e-mail, country, mailbox credentials, sent requests and the evidence log are stored only on your computer, encrypted. The encryption key is protected by Windows (DPAPI) and tied to your user. The Licensor has no access to it.

2. Data that goes through the Licensor or its providers.

3. E-mail. The program connects to your mail provider with the credentials you enter. Reading the mailbox (the "find out what I'm subscribed to" function) is read-only and local: messages are neither copied nor sent to third parties.

4. Data sent to services. When you confirm a request, the program sends the chosen service your name, your e-mail and the text of the request, from your mailbox.

5. Retention and deletion. Data stays on your computer until you delete it with "Delete all my data" or uninstall the program and remove the %LOCALAPPDATA%\cleancli folder.

6. Your rights. For the data in section 2 you may exercise the rights in arts. 15-22 GDPR by writing to mirenlock.support@gmail.com and lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country.
